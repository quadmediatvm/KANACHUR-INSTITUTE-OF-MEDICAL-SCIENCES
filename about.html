About Kanachur Islamic Education Trust

A charitable non-profit trust dedicated to inclusive education, societal upliftment, and holistic healthcare across a 70-acre campus in Natekal, Mangaluru.

Founding Philosophy

Inclusive Opportunity & Lifetime Educational Pathway

Kanachur Islamic Education Trust (R), the sponsoring body, was founded in the year 2001 by Dr. Haji U.K. Monu. It is a family trust where Dr. Haji U.K. Monu serves as the Founder Trustee, alongside Trustees Mrs. Zohara Monu, Mr. Abdul Rahiman, Mrs. Umaya Banu, Mrs. Farida Monu, and Dr. Abida Monu.

The prime objective of this non-profit charitable trust is to provide inclusive education and create equal opportunities for students from all strata of society. A child joining Kanachur at kindergarten can progress through elementary, primary, secondary, and pre-university levels, into higher professional healthcare and medical degrees—all within the same serene campus.

The trust actively supports deserving students from economically weaker sections by providing fee concessions, scholarships, and essential educational materials to enable them to complete formal education rooted in moral principles and human values.

Natekal Campus Environment

70-Acre Eco-Campus & Western Ghats Biodiversity Garden

Situated in Natekal, Ullal taluk, overlooking the Western Ghats and Netravati River connected to the Arabian Sea.

🌄

Geographic Serenity

Located in rural Mangaluru, the campus enjoys sea and land breezes year-round, maintaining comfortable mean temperatures (25.1°C to 31.4°C) with rich annual rainfall of 2453 mm.

🌱

1000 Plant Species Garden

In collaboration with scientists from Pilikula Bio University Park, the campus features a biodiversity garden with over 1,000 native plant species of the Western Ghats with botanical labeling.

💧

Rainwater Harvesting

Demonstrating environmental conservation through roof rainwater harvesting, borewell recharging, and clay-lined pond water retention across the 70-acre campus.
