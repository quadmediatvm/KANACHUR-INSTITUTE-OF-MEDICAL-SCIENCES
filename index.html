MISSION STATEMENT

Promoting Higher Values & Ethics

To actively promote and preserve the higher values and ethics in Medical education. We pursue excellence while consciously meeting the expectations of the people we serve without prejudice and in all fairness to stay socially meaningful in propagation of arts and sciences to serve humanity at large.
