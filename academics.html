Degrees & Qualifications

Our Affiliated Academic Degree Programs

Affiliated with Rajiv Gandhi University of Health Sciences (RGUHS) & recognized by national apex councils.

KIMS MEDICAL

MBBS (Bachelor of Medicine & Surgery)

5.5 Years degree with 1-year compulsory rotating internship at the 500-bed Kanachur Hospital. Intensive clinical rotations across 20+ specialties.

Affiliation: RGUHS / NMC Recognized
Duration: 4.5 Years + 1 Year Internship
KCONS NURSING

B.Sc Nursing & M.Sc Nursing

Comprehensive clinical nursing training combining bedside practice with simulation labs. Celebrating 10 years of nursing education excellence.

Affiliation: INC & KNC Approved
Duration: 4 Years (UG) / 2 Years (PG)
KCOP PHYSIOTHERAPY

BPT (Bachelor of Physiotherapy)

Advanced education in physical rehabilitation, sports medicine, neuro-rehab, and electrotherapy with live clinical patient interactions.

Affiliation: RGUHS Affiliated
Duration: 4.5 Years
KAMC AYURVEDA

BAMS (Ayurvedacharya)

Traditional Indian medicine integrated with modern diagnostic science. Features field study at the campus 1000-species botanical garden.

Affiliation: NCISM / Ayush Ministry
Duration: 5.5 Years
KIAHS ALLIED HEALTH

B.Sc MLT & Optometry

High-demand technical degrees in Medical Laboratory Technology, Ophthalmic Optometry, and Diagnostic Imaging Technology.

Affiliation: RGUHS Recognized
Duration: 3.5 to 4 Years
PHARMACY

B.Pharm & D.Pharm

Pharmaceutical formulation, clinical trial analysis, drug safety, and community pharmacy practice with modern research laboratories.

Affiliation: PCI Approved
Duration: 2 Years (Diploma) / 4 Years (Degree)
